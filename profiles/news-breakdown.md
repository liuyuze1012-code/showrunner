# Profile — News breakdown

**Something happened; explain what it means.** Speed matters, so the framework
is trimmed: fewer gates, tighter structure, heavier compliance.

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 3–8 min |
| Turnaround | Hours, not days |
| Presenter | Usually on camera |
| Backbone | Event → context → implication |

## Structure

```
0–5s     what happened, stated flatly. No throat-clearing
~10s     why it matters to this viewer specifically
body     what happened → the context you're missing → what it means next
close    what to watch for, with a date or trigger if possible
```

Do not bury the event. A news viewer who doesn't know what happened by second
five leaves.

## Trigger table preset

| # | Speaker is… | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stating what happened | Headline card, full screen |
| 2 | Giving a date or timeline | Timeline, built progressively |
| 3 | Quoting an official statement | Quote card with attribution **on screen** |
| 4 | Naming a company or person | Logo / portrait chip |
| 5 | Giving a figure | Stat card + source chip |
| 6 | Describing something filmable | Archive footage, era-matched |
| 7 | Comparing to a precedent | Split screen |
| 8 | **Speculating** | **On-screen "analysis / not confirmed" label** |
| 9 | Emphasizing a word | Accent keyword |
| 10 | Concluding | Beat zoom |

Row 8 is this profile's defining rule. The line between *reporting* and
*speculating* must be visible, not just audible — it's what protects you when
a prediction turns out wrong.

## Gate emphasis

- **Gate A collapses.** The news chose the topic.
- **Gate B stays.** Speed is not a reason to skip fact-checking; it's the
  reason you need it.
- **Gate E is usually off.** Generated imagery of a real recent event is a
  credibility problem, not a shortcut. Use archive footage or graphics.

## Compliance

This profile carries the most risk in the framework. See
[`05-compliance.md`](../framework/05-compliance.md).

- **Never illustrate a real event with generated imagery.** Not once. Whatever
  you label it, a synthetic image of a real news event will be screenshotted
  without the label.
- Attribute every claim on screen, not just in the voiceover.
- Use official terminology for incidents; pair a specific event with general
  context in the same breath.
- Corrections policy: decide in advance how you'll correct an error, and say so.

## Failure modes

- **Speculation drift.** Paragraph one is sourced, paragraph four is opinion,
  and nothing on screen marked the transition.
- **Stale footage.** Reusing B-roll from the last three videos on the same
  subject. Viewers notice faster here than anywhere else.
- **Hedging into uselessness.** "It's too early to say" is only worth saying
  once, and only with what *would* settle it.
