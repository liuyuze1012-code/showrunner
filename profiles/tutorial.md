# Profile — Tutorial

**The viewer follows along and completes a task.** Success is measurable: did
it work for them? That makes this the most testable profile here.

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 3–20 min |
| Presenter | Voiceover over screen or hands, often no face |
| Backbone | Sequential steps |

## Structure

```
0–10s    the finished result. Show what they'll have at the end
~20s     prerequisites — what they need before starting
body     numbered steps. One action per step. Real time on the hard parts
close    verification: how to know it worked. Common failure and its fix
```

**Show the result first.** A viewer who can't see where they're going won't
follow you there.

## Trigger table preset

| # | Speaker is… | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Starting a new step** | **Step number, persistent on screen** |
| 2 | Naming an exact value, command or setting | On-screen text, **copyable and exact** |
| 3 | Pointing at something | Highlight, arrow or zoom on **that** element |
| 4 | Warning about a mistake | Warning card, accent color |
| 5 | Doing something fast | Slow down or repeat. Never speed up a critical action |
| 6 | Listing prerequisites | Checklist, built progressively |
| 7 | Branching ("if you're on Mac…") | Branch label, held for the whole branch |
| 8 | Finishing a step | Brief confirmation beat |
| 9 | Emphasizing a word | Accent keyword |
| 10 | **Explaining why** | **Nothing.** Clean screen. Let the reasoning breathe |

Row 1 is non-negotiable: a persistent step number is what lets someone who
paused for ten minutes rejoin without scrubbing.

## Gate emphasis

- **Gate B must be executed, not just read.** Follow your own written steps on
  a clean machine before recording. Every tutorial error is a step the author
  had already done and forgot to mention.
- **Gate F adds a completion test:** could a stranger follow this and succeed?
- **Gate E is almost always off.** Generated imagery has no place in a
  procedure. Screen recordings and real footage only.

## Accuracy

- **Version-stamp everything.** Software changes; the video doesn't. State the
  version on screen and in the description.
- **Exact strings must be exact.** Commands, paths, settings names. One wrong
  character means a stranger's failed afternoon.
- **State the failure modes.** "If you see this error, it's because…" is often
  the most valuable thirty seconds in the video.

## Failure modes

- **Expert blindness.** Skipping the step you do automatically. This is the
  number-one tutorial defect and the reason for the execute-your-own-steps rule.
- **Speeding up the hard part** because it's boring to watch. It's boring
  because it's difficult, which is why they came.
- **No verification step.** The viewer doesn't know whether they succeeded.
- **Burying prerequisites** at minute four, after they've started.
