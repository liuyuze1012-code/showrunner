# Profiles

Starting points by video type. Each gives you a trigger table preset, a format
skeleton, gate emphasis, and the failure modes specific to that genre.

**These are starting points, not templates.** The
[tailoring interview](../framework/00-tailor.md) picks the closest one and
then modifies it from your answers. Nobody should ship the preset unchanged.

| Profile | Best for | Typical length |
|---|---|---|
| [Explainer / video essay](explainer.md) | One subject argued to the bottom | 5–30 min |
| [News breakdown](news-breakdown.md) | Fast turnaround on a current event | 3–8 min |
| [Interview / podcast cutdown](interview.md) | Long conversations → shorts and episodes | 60s – 40 min |
| [Product review](product-review.md) | Evaluating a thing against criteria | 5–15 min |
| [Tutorial](tutorial.md) | Teaching a procedure someone will follow | 3–20 min |
| [Documentary short](documentary.md) | Narrative built from footage and interviews | 8–30 min |

## Picking

Choose by **what the video does**, not by subject matter. A video about a
telescope can be an explainer, a review, or a documentary — those are three
different builds.

Ask: *what does the viewer have at the end?*

- Understanding of a subject → **explainer**
- Understanding of an event that just happened → **news breakdown**
- Access to someone's thinking → **interview**
- A purchase decision → **product review**
- A completed task → **tutorial**
- An experience → **documentary**

## What if none fit?

Start from the closest and cut what doesn't apply. The transferable structure
is the gates, the learning loop, and the *shape* of a trigger table — not any
specific row in it.
