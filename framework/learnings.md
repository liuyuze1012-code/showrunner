# Learnings — inbox

> **This file stays near-empty.** It is an inbox, not an archive.
>
> New feedback is appended here in the format below. When it passes roughly 20
> entries — or on request — run a consolidation pass: fold entries into the
> canonical specs *in their own language*, supersede replaced rules with a
> dated note, empty this file, and commit.
>
> Protocol: [`02-learning-loop.md`](02-learning-loop.md)

---

## Template

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — <one-line title>
- **Rule:** what to do or not do. Specific enough to act on without interpreting.
- **Why:** the reasoning, or the incident behind it.
- **How to apply:** when it fires, what it interacts with, what it does not cover.
```

All three parts are mandatory. A rule without its reasoning cannot be safely
revised at the next consolidation — it can only be obeyed forever or deleted
carelessly.

If a new rule **contradicts an existing spec**, do not resolve it. Record both
and escalate to the showrunner.
